About Me

Thanks for stopping by! I'm Tan Tse Hao.

Since my first contact with programming, I've been fascinated by solving coding problems and solving problems with code. Over the years, I've come to realise how deep the rabbit hole of software engineering goes; I haven't escaped since. I'm grateful to have found a professional calling in a field that offers endless opportunities for learning and growth.

Before my software engineering career, I once represented Malaysia in the World Schools Debating Championship in 2013. I graduated from Monash University Malaysia with a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechatronics and was awarded the Best Graduate Award (Nov 2020) for my academic performance.

Outside of software, I love learning about and discussing various topics from philosophy, economics, mathematics, and science. English is my native language, but I also have competencies in Mandarin, Malay, and Japanese. I enjoy badminton, cardistry, and drumming. Finally, I would not be where I am without my friends and family, and spending time with them gives life depth and purpose.
